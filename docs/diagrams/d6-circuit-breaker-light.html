Enterprise Fault Tolerance & Reliability

Model Pool Cascade & Per-Member Circuit Breakers

Every Route tries the Model Pool in order, each member under its own pybreaker circuit breaker. A rate limit, quota error, 5xx or timeout moves to the next member at once; any member's answer is a normal, attributed answer. Only when no member answers is the degraded Redis FAQ answer served. One breaker per member serves both buffered and streaming paths (ADR-0004).
